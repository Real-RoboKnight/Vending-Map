SnackScout

Welcome to SnackScout, the crowdsourced vending machine and snack finder for the hungry world. Take a look at our Map in the navigation bar and search in the search bar for any snack. Don't see the snack you crave? Why not contribute data with our nifty Contribute Data tab? Powered by Google Gemini, any photo you take will be scanned and sent to our MongoDB database which you can then search for immediately.
